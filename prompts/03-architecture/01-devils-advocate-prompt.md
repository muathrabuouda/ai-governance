# Devil's Advocate Prompt — Architectural Critique

**Software Architecture** · "Architectural Decisions Cannot Be Delegated" · Sceptical Review

```text
Here is an Architecture Decision Record (ADR) written for our project, along with our non-functional requirements.
You are a sceptical software architect. Do not agree with me.
1. What are the three strongest arguments against this architectural decision?
2. What alternative did I fail to evaluate that I should have evaluated?
3. Under what conditions will this decision prove wrong within one year?
4. What negative consequence or operational trade-off did I fail to document?
```
