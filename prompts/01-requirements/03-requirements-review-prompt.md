# Requirements Sceptical Review Prompt

**Requirements Engineering** · "Prompts and Acceptance Criteria" · Principle: Find the specification gaps before writing code.

```text
Review this specification from the perspective of a sceptical QA and test engineer.
1. Flag every acceptance criterion that cannot be automated in tests, and propose an automatable formulation.
2. Identify every ambiguous word or subjective phrase (e.g., "fast", "user-friendly", "reasonable").
3. Identify five edge cases or failure modes not covered.
4. Highlight any contradiction between two requirements.
Do not praise and do not rewrite the document; provide only the numbered critique list.
```
