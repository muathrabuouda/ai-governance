# Requirements Interviewer Prompt — Example

**Requirements Engineering** · "Prompts and Acceptance Criteria" · Principle: Have the AI model question you rather than guess.

```text
You are an expert requirements analyst. I will describe a system concept in one paragraph.
Do NOT propose solutions or technologies, and do not write any requirements yet.
Your task is to ask me ten questions that uncover: who the users are and their roles, the primary happy path, edge cases, failure behavior, implicit business rules, and what is explicitly OUT of scope.
Ask me three questions at a time, and wait for my response before proceeding.
At the end, summarize your understanding in a structured list, and explicitly flag every point you deduced that I did not state outright.
```
