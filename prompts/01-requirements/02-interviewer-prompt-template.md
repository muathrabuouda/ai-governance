# Requirements Interviewer Prompt — Workshop Template

**Requirements Engineering** · "Prompts and Acceptance Criteria" · Workshop Template: Interactive Discovery

```text
You are an expert requirements analyst. I will describe a system concept in one paragraph.
Do NOT propose solutions or technologies, and do not write any requirements yet.
Ask me ten questions that uncover: users and their roles, the primary happy path, edge cases, failure behavior, implicit business rules, and what is OUT of scope.
Ask me three questions at a time, and wait for my response.
At the end, summarize your understanding and explicitly flag every point you deduced that I did not state outright.

System Concept: [Paste your system idea here]
```
